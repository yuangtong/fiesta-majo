# Prompts: títulos en letras globo (IA)

Reemplazan a Spline (más liviano, sin sello "Built with Spline", funciona en todos los celulares).
Al tenerlos, se guardan en `public/images/titulos/` y se ponen en `event.config.js → titulos`.

## 1. "Huamanga Club"
```text
The words "Huamanga Club" written in inflated chrome/foil party balloon letters (like silver mylar
letter balloons with soft iridescent blue-pink reflections, visible seams and crinkles, glossy highlights).
Two lines: "Huamanga" on the first line, "Club" centered on the second line. Letters slightly overlapping,
playful, bubbly, perfectly legible (the "u" must not look like a "v").
Isolated on a plain solid #FF00FF magenta background, no shadow on the background, no other objects.
Front view, high quality 3D render, 2048x2048.
```

## 2. "Estás invitado"
```text
The words "Estás invitado" written in inflated glossy party balloon letters, same style as the reference
(chrome/foil mylar balloons, soft iridescent reflections, seams and crinkles). Two lines: "Estás" on top,
"invitado" below. The accent on the "á" must be visible (a small balloon tick). Perfectly legible.
Isolated on a plain solid #FF00FF magenta background, no shadow on the background, no other objects.
Front view, high quality 3D render, 2048x2048.
```

Tips: usar la captura de las letras de Spline o la nube como referencia de estilo. Si la IA escribe mal
alguna letra, regenerar solo esa variación. El magenta lo quito yo.
